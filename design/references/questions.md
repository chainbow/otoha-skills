# Questions to ask

Ask at most four, in one message, each with your own suggestion so the user can answer "ok". Ask in the user's language, in everyday words. Skip any the request already answers. If you cannot ask, take the suggestion and say so afterwards.

| Need to know | How to ask | Default if unanswered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose and place | "What is it for, and where will people see it: printed, on a phone, on a screen?" | Poster: printed A3 portrait. Social: a phone feed. Screen mockup: a phone. |
| Audience | "Who should it speak to?" | The people the subject already names (diners, customers, colleagues). |
| Size | "Which size: A3 poster, a square for social media, or a phone screen?" | See `sizes.md`. Choose by the place above. |
| Look | "Any colours, logo or style to follow? For example calm and natural, bold and loud, clean and technical." | Pick one clear mood from the subject (a bakery: warm and friendly; a bank app: calm and precise). |
| Words and facts | "What must it say: title, date, place, price, contact?" | Invent short, plausible words only for titles and taglines; never invent dates, prices, phone numbers or names. Leave those as obvious placeholders ("日期待定") and tell the user. |
| Pictures | "Do you have a photo or logo, or should I draw simple shapes?" | Shapes and type only. |

## By kind of work

- **Poster or flyer**: what is the one thing to do or remember (come on Saturday, buy the new product)? Is there a date, place, price?
- **App or web screen**: who uses it and what do they do first on this screen? Which device? Which states matter (empty, loading, error)? Light or dark?
- **Infographic**: what is the one conclusion? Where do the numbers come from, and as of when? Never make up numbers; if the user gave none, ask or label the figures as examples.
- **Flow or diagram**: where does it start and end? Who or what takes each step? Which steps branch?
- **Prototype with several screens**: which path should be shown from start to finish? Keep it to the screens on that path.

## After asking

State your assumptions in the final message in one sentence ("按 A3 竖版、暖色调做的，日期和地址先留了占位"). The user can then correct one thing rather than start over.
